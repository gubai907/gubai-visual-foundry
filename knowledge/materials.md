# Materials

皮肤：低对比不规则纹理、轻微自然变化，不用印纹/迷宫式毛孔或统一塑料表面。
发丝：分束、发际衔接和少量自然碎发，不凭空改变发型。
织物：织纹、厚度、接缝、重力和张力；皮革：合理纹理与折痕；金属：有控制的反射和边缘，结构不能融化。
只针对可见材质加载描述，不给纯平面海报加毛孔。不凭视觉推断商品成分或新增刻字、缝线。

## 可复制的人体真实感片段

按可见区域选 1–2 项，不全部堆入同一 Prompt。

- Face/skin：`realistic low-contrast irregular skin detail, subtle pores with natural variation, slight uneven tone, gentle natural asymmetry, believable hairline and a few fine flyaway hairs, restrained makeup, relaxed present expression`
- Hands：`realistic hand anatomy, one thumb and four fingers per visible hand, natural knuckle lines, subtle tendons and joints, visible nail beds, short natural nails, fine skin creases, physically plausible grip and contact`
- Neck/shoulder/torso：`anatomically normal adult neck length and width, relaxed trapezius muscles, continuous jaw-to-neck-to-clavicle-to-shoulder transition, natural posture and physically plausible garment contact`
- Garment：`physically constructed garment with believable seams, neckline, armholes, fabric weave, thickness, gravity and tension; no melted fabric or impossible cut lines`

“一拇指四手指”只约束完整可见手，不要求把自然遮挡的手指强行露出。

## 按风险选择 Negative

- 基础质感：`plastic skin, poreless face, fingerprint-like texture, maze-like pores, embossed skin, oversharpening, uncanny expression`
- 人体/手部：`identity drift, copied face, celebrity likeness, extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, duplicated hands, warped knuckles, detached thumb, broken wrist, elongated thin neck, disconnected jaw-neck-shoulder anatomy`
- 产品/对象：`altered product/object design, floating object, object embedded in skin, broken attachment, product/object out of focus, distorted key detail`
- 服装/编辑：`impossible garment seams, melted fabric, repainted protected patch, altered source perspective, changed contact shadow`

负面项必须与正向要求相容，并且只选当前风险。
