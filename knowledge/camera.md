# Camera

先确定相机高度、距离、视角、景别和清晰读取区，再选一组焦段/景深语言。参数描述视觉行为，不保证生成器执行真实光学模拟。
人脸避免无意广角变形，全身避免过低机位拉长腿缩头；产品近景让边缘、连接和接触处清晰。
## 摄影预设

| 用途 | 可复制控制 |
|---|---|
| 身份人物正面/45° | `eye-level portrait perspective, 80–90mm equivalent lens language, medium camera distance, f/5.6 depth-of-field behavior, natural facial proportions without wide-angle distortion, complete facial structure clearly readable` |
| 完整侧脸 | `eye-level full-profile portrait, 85–105mm equivalent lens language, f/5.6–f/8 depth-of-field behavior, forehead, nose bridge, lips, chin, ear, jaw, neck and shoulder transition clearly readable, no compressed or elongated anatomy` |
| 产品与人物近景 | `90–105mm macro-equivalent close portrait, f/8 depth-of-field behavior, complete product/object edge, attachment and contact relationship in precise focus, no shallow-focus blur hiding structure` |
| 半身生活方式 | `natural eye-level half-body perspective, 50–70mm equivalent lens language, f/4–f/5.6 depth-of-field behavior, believable person-to-environment scale, recognizable material cues, no wide-angle limb or facial distortion` |
| 物品微距 | `90–120mm macro-equivalent product perspective, f/8–f/11 depth-of-field behavior, complete product/object structure and connection points sharp from front edge to rear edge, controlled highlights, accurate proportions` |

预设只在解决透视、景深、产品读取或人体比例时使用；数值描述视觉行为，不是跨工具的真实光学保证。
每图选择一套，不同时列多个相机/焦段；不要用虚化藏掉结构错误。已有源机位锁定或洗图时不更换镜头。
